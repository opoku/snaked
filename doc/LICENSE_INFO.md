## License Choice Rationale

The MIT License was chosen for the Snaked project for the following reasons:

1. **Permissive Nature**: The MIT License allows anyone to use, modify, and distribute the software with minimal restrictions, encouraging widespread adoption and contribution.

2. **Simplicity**: The MIT License is short, easy to understand, and straightforward to comply with.

3. **Commercial Use**: The license permits commercial use of the software, making it suitable for both academic and commercial applications.

4. **Compatibility**: The MIT License is compatible with most other open source licenses, making it easy to integrate Snaked with other projects.

5. **Limited Liability**: The license includes clear disclaimers of warranty and liability, protecting contributors from legal issues.
